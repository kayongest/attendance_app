2. The 4-Tier Security Clearance Matrix (L1 – L4)

Access privileges within the venue are governed by four distinct security clearance levels. Each tier enforces clear perimeter boundaries, turnstile rules, badge color coding, and administrative capabilities.

Level 1 (L1) Event Venue Basic Crew
Roles: General Venue Crew, Catering, Cleaners, Hospitality, Drivers, Security.
Clearance Scope: Front of House, General Access, Perimeter Gates.
High-Security Zones: RESTRICTED (Stage, FOH & Control Rooms)
  • Authorized for staff entrance turnstiles and perimeter gates only.
  • Turnstile scans at backstage and control room doors trigger "ACCESS RESTRICTED: L1 BASIC".
  • Standard shift attendance logging; zero administrative or override privileges.
  • Subject to basic shift attendance recording and check-out verification.
Level 2 (L2) Technical & Production Crew
Roles: Sound Engineers, Lighting Techs, Video Directors, Riggers, Stage Managers.
Clearance Scope: Full Venue Production Perimeters, Backstage, Stage Left/Right, FOH.
Call-Time Enforcement: STRICT 4-STATE PUNCTUALITY
  • Unrestricted movement across live production zones, truss access points, and technical booths.
  • Bound to strict scheduled Call-Times: Auto-disabled if arriving > 15 min late.
  • Requires in-person supervisor override to restore access if tardy.
  • Profile links to punctuality scorecards, show assignments, and department rosters.
Level 3 (L3) Gate Supervisors & Attendance Controllers
Roles: Gate Kiosk Operators, Check-In Desk Marshals, Turnstile Supervisors.
Clearance Scope: Access Checkpoints, Security Gates, Loading Docks.
Gate Authority: TERMINAL OPERATOR & VERIFIER
  • Operates Gate Kiosks (#pane-kiosk), executes RFID scans, verifies photo matches.
  • Monitors turnstile flow and flags badge anomalies or visual mismatches.
  • Separation of Duties: Strictly prohibited from creating or modifying crew accounts (prevents internal credential fabrication).
  • Registers manual check-in/out under supervisor supervision when badges are damaged.
Level 4 (L4) Executive & Operations Management
Roles: Technical Leads, Production Directors, Operations Managers, Administrators.
Clearance Scope: Universal 24/7 All-Access across all doors, gates, and perimeters.
Governance: CALL-TIMES, OVERRIDES & HR APPROVALS
  • Defines event Call-Times, shift schedules, and grace periods.
  • Empowered with Emergency Late Override to reactivate disabled badges.
  • Issues advance "With Permission" exemption passes for unavoidable delays.
  • Reviews and approves/rejects "Missing / Proof" submissions; certifies payroll.

Separation of Duties: Anti-Fraud Architecture

Why Gate Controllers (L3) Cannot Create Users or Execute Overrides Alone
To prevent gate collusion (where a turnstile marshal secretly admits friends or fake employees), Gate Controllers (L3) are physically locked out from user creation and late override permissions. Overriding a disabled badge requires a two-party interaction: the technician at the gate and an authenticated Level 4 supervisor logging the override with individual credentials.

4. Dedicated Technician Attendance & Punctuality Lifecycle

Live event production is hyper-sensitive to call-time punctuality. If a Front of House audio engineer or lighting tech arrives late, rehearsals stall, sound checks fail, and show schedules crumble. The aBility system enforces an automated 4-State Punctuality State Machine:

State 1
ON TIME
Arrival within call-time window (+15m grace). Full green badge admission. Shift active.
State 2
LATE (DISABLE)
Arrival past cutoff (>15m late). Badge locked at gate. In-person supervisor unlock required.
State 3
WITH PERMISSION
Pre-approved late pass or shift adjustment. Admitted cleanly with audit attribution.
State 4
MISSING / PROOF
No-show at shift cutoff. Hard suspension until verified documentary proof uploaded.
STATE 1: ON TIME Punctual Check-In & Immediate Gate Clearance
GREEN PASS • ACTIVE

Qualification Rule: Technician scans at any authorized venue checkpoint between 60 minutes prior to Call-Time up to 15 minutes after Call-Time (Standard Grace Period).

System & Turnstile Behavior:

  • Instant acoustic chime and green terminal banner: "ACCESS GRANTED — ON TIME".
  • Attendance log record generated with access_status = 'granted' and punctuality_status = 'on_time'.
  • Workforce state transitions from Ready to Checked In with timestamp down to the second.
  • Full access rights across assigned production perimeters (Backstage, Stage Left/Right, FOH).
STATE 2: LATE (DISABLE) Automated Access Suspension & Supervisor Override Gate
GATE LOCKOUT • HARD STOP

Qualification Rule: Technician scans > 15 minutes after the scheduled Call-Time with zero pre-authorized exemption on record.

System & Turnstile Behavior:

  • Automatic Badge Disable: Turnstile physically rejects entry. Terminal flashes red visual alert: "ACCESS RESTRICTED: LATE CALL-TIME — BADGE LOCKED".
  • Prevents late technicians from quietly slipping onto live stages, bypassing safety briefings, or billing unworked hours.
  • Supervisor Unlock Protocol: To admit the technician, a Level 4 supervisor (Technical Lead or Operations Manager) must log into the supervisor terminal, enter authorization credentials, document the reason for tardiness, and execute an "Override & Admit" command.
  • The incident is permanently stamped in the audit log, recording the exact minutes of tardiness and the supervisor who authorized entry.
STATE 3: WITH PERMISSION Pre-Authorized Schedule Variance & Protected Scorecard
APPROVED OVERRIDE • NO PENALTY

Qualification Rule: Technician notified production management in advance (e.g., flight delay, transit disruption, sound check rescheduling, medical appointment) and received an authorized exemption pass.

System & Turnstile Behavior:

  • Supervisor issues a "Permission Token" in the management portal with expected arrival time and reason.
  • When the technician presents their badge at the gate, the terminal reads the active permission token.
  • Kiosk projects: "ACCESS GRANTED — WITH PERMISSION (Authorized by Tech Lead)".
  • Technician is admitted without delay; punctuality logs record with_permission, protecting the technician's reliability scorecard and payroll contract from penalty deductions.
STATE 4: MISSING — PROVIDE PROOF Unaccounted No-Show & Mandatory Documentary Evidence Workflow
SUSPENSION • PROOF AUDIT

Qualification Rule: Scheduled technician fails to scan at any venue gate before the shift call-time cutoff, with no pre-approved permission logged.

System & Turnstile Behavior:

  • Automated background job flags the technician profile as MISSING / NO-SHOW.
  • Immediate alert sent to the Technical Lead to dispatch a backup/stand-in technician so show operations are not left uncrewed.
  • Hard Perimeter Suspension: The technician's badge is blacklisted from all venue gates.
  • Mandatory Proof Submission: To clear the flag and authorize hours, the technician must submit official documentary evidence (hospital note, official transit delay slip, police incident log).
  • HR / Management reviews proof -> If Approved: Tagged as "Excused Absence (Proof Verified)" and badge restored; If Rejected: Confirmed as "Unexcused Absence (Disciplinary Flag)" with contracted penalties.

Anti-Passback & Real-Time Perimeter Enforcement

Anti-Passback Security Rule: Preventing Badge Hand-Backs
The aBility state machine enforces strict Anti-Passback logic: Once a badge is scanned as Checked In, it cannot be scanned again for entry at any gate until a corresponding Checked Out scan is recorded. If a technician attempts to hand their badge backward over the turnstile to an accomplice, the terminal immediately alarms: "DENIED: BADGE ALREADY CHECKED IN".

Offline Local Resilience & Network Redundancy

Production venues often experience network dropouts. The aBility Gate Kiosk operates with an intelligent local caching engine on XAMPP/MySQL:

  • If external internet drops, the local gate server continues processing scans, verifying headshots, and enforcing call-time rules without interruption.
  • Once network connectivity resumes, all attendance logs synchronize automatically to the central management database with original timestamps preserved.
