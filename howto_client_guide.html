1. Getting Started & Signing In

Every team member signs into the portal using credentials issued by your organization's Administrator or Site Manager. The interface automatically adapts to your assigned role so frontline operators stay focused while managers retain full oversight.

1
Open the Portal & Sign In

Navigate to your deployment URL (login.php), enter your username or email and password, and submit to launch the active workspace (index.php).

2
Use the Top Bar Utilities Anytime

Use ⌘K / Ctrl+K for quick staff/badge search, click the Theme Toggle (Alt+T) to switch between Light and Dark mode, open Gate Activity Alerts (bell icon) for real-time notifications, or click your profile avatar to inspect your badge or switch operators.

3
Launch the Built-In Interactive Guided Tour

Click the Help (?) icon in the top-right bar and select Interactive Guided Tour to run a live spotlight walkthrough of either the Gate Dashboard or the Gate Terminal.

Who Sees What? (Role-Based Workspace Scope)

Role Primary Screens Key Capabilities
Administrator / Director All Screens (Dashboard, Venues, Users & Roles, Badges, Terminals, Facilities, Matrix) Full system configuration, user & clearance tier management (L1–L4), site PIN configuration, shift relief approvals, exports, and Reset Today’s Scans.
Site / Stock Manager Dashboard, Facilities & Gates, Users (L1–L3 Scope), Terminals, Shift Relief Manage facility gates and operating hours, onboard facility crew (L1–L2) and Gate Controllers (L3), view site PINs, and approve shift relief requests.
Technical Lead / PM Dashboard, Venues & Checkpoints, Users (Crew Scope), Terminals Adjust event/venue call-times, monitor live roll-call, inspect late arrivals, and coordinate technical crew coverage.
Gate Controller
(Tier L3 Operator)		 Terminals (#pane-kiosk) & Staff ID Badges Locked to their assigned gate checkpoint for distraction-free badge scanning, photo verification, camera QR scanning, and submitting shift relief requests.
First-Time Operator Tip
If you are signed in as a Gate Controller, the system automatically routes you directly to your assigned Gate Terminal so you do not have to search through administrative menus during busy crew check-in rushes.

2. Operating the Gate Terminal (#pane-kiosk)

The Terminals screen is the frontline check-in/check-out station used at warehouses, loading bays, and event venue doors. Every terminal opens in a secure locked state until unlocked with the checkpoint's dedicated PIN.

Step-by-Step: Unlocking & Scanning Crew

1
Unlock the Terminal Screen

On entry, the terminal displays Terminal Screen Locked with the active facility name. Enter the checkpoint's dedicated Site Terminal PIN (provided by your Administrator or Site Manager) and click Unlock Terminal.

2
Verify Checkpoint & Scan Mode

Confirm the selected type (Stock Warehouse or Event Venue) and target checkpoint. Keep Scan Mode on Auto (Smart Check-In / Check-Out) for normal operations, or switch to Force Check-In Only, Force Check-Out Only, or Verify Access Only when needed.

3
Scan a Staff Badge (3 Ways)

A. USB Barcode / RFID Scanner: Press / to focus the input field, scan the card, and press Enter.
B. Tablet / Phone Camera: Click Camera Scan, align the staff QR code or barcode inside the green reticle frame, and use Flip Camera to switch front/rear lenses.
C. Manual Entry: Type the badge code (e.g., REW-BADGE-0006, ABL-006) or staff name and click Verify & Record.

4
Confirm Visual Photo & Equipment Custody

The Verification Modal displays the person's headshot, full name, clearance badge, punctuality result, and any linked equipment batches. Visually match the face to the person at the gate to prevent buddy-punching.

5
Lock the Terminal When Stepping Away

Click Lock Screen in the terminal header whenever leaving a kiosk unattended. Scanning and checkpoint switching stay disabled until the Site PIN is entered again.

Managing Site Terminal PINs

Administrators and Site Managers can click Site PINs on the terminal header (or configure PINs when editing a Facility Gate) to inspect or update each checkpoint's dedicated unlock PIN.

Anti-Passback Protection

In Auto mode, a checked-in badge toggles cleanly to Checked Out on the next scan. In Force Check-In, attempting to check in an already checked-in badge is blocked to stop card hand-backs.

3. Call-Times, Punctuality & Access Rules

Every scan is automatically evaluated against two live engines: Security Clearance Rules (whether the badge tier is authorized at that checkpoint) and Call-Time Punctuality (whether the crew member arrived on schedule).

How to Set or Extend Call-Time During Live Operations

  • Quick Extensions on the Gate Terminal: In the Active Shift Call-Time bar above the scanner, click +15m, +30m, or +1h to extend the shift call-time immediately, or click Reset to return to the baseline schedule.
  • Full Call-Time Configuration: Click Edit on the quickbar or select Set Call Time in the left sidebar to choose any checkpoint and enter an exact time. All subsequent scans evaluate against the updated time (with a standard 15-minute grace window).

Understanding Scan & Punctuality Statuses

Status When It Triggers What the Gate Operator Should Do
Early / On-Time Badge checks in before Call-Time or within the +15-minute grace period. Verify photo match and admit immediately. Shift clock starts cleanly.
Late Arrival Badge scans after the Call-Time + 15-minute grace window expires. Confirm whether the late arrival has supervisor authorization or log the variance per site policy.
Checked Out Second scan at shift end (or in Force Check-Out mode). Confirm any assigned equipment batches are accounted for before departure.
Access Denied Inactive/suspended badge, closed facility hours, or insufficient clearance tier for that checkpoint. Do not admit. Check the denial reason displayed on-screen and escalate to the Site Manager or Admin.

4-Tier Physical Clearance Overview

Tier Role Group Default Checkpoint Access
Level 1 Venue Crew Standard crew access for assigned Event Venues and general operational support during scheduled call-times.
Level 2 Technical & Stage Audio, lighting, video, and stage technicians with access to Event Venues, FOH/Backstage, and designated Stock dispatch gates.
Level 3 Stock & Gate Control Warehouse controllers and logistics operators with full access to Stock Warehouses, loading bays, and assigned venues.
Level 4 Executive / All-Access Unrestricted 24/7 entry across all Stock Warehouses, production vaults, backstage zones, and Event Venues.

4. Live Dashboard, Allocation Map & Roll-Call

Operations leads and Administrators use the Gate Dashboard (Gate Operations & Access Matrix) to monitor real-time headcount, inspect gate traffic, and verify who is physically inside every venue or facility.

Using the Live Dashboard

  1. Monitor the 4 Live KPI Cards: Track Active Badges, Currently On-Site, Daily Check-Ins, and Late / Denied Exceptions in real time.
  2. Inspect the User Activity Timeline: Use the search box to find any technician, venue, or gate, and use the scope dropdown (All Logs, Venues Only, Facility Gates Only, Access Granted, Access Denied) to narrow down events.
  3. Open an Audit Record: Click any row in the User Activity Timeline to open the Gate Activity & Transit Audit Record modal, where you can review scan timestamps, operator attribution, and any active transit workflow—and click history rows to switch between records.

Real-Time Personnel & Facility Allocation Map

Positioned beside the Activity Timeline, the Allocation Map visualizes where crew members are currently checked in or in transit across active venues and logistics hubs:

  • Switch Views: Toggle between the spatial Map layout and the Traffic view to inspect movement across checkpoints.
  • Allocate & Dispatch Personnel: Supervisors can click a facility/dispatch control to assign or dispatch crew members directly to a target checkpoint.

Emergency On-Site Crew Roll-Call

How to Pull an Instant On-Site Headcount
Click On-Site Roll-Call in the left sidebar (or click the Currently On-Site KPI card, or select it from the Help (?) menu). The modal lists everyone currently checked inside the perimeter along with their checkpoint, role, and emergency contact details—ready for rapid muster verification during drills or incidents.

5. Offline Scan Queue & Night-Shift Reports

Live production environments—such as underground loading docks or outdoor festival grounds—can lose Wi-Fi or cellular signal. aBility Manager keeps gate operations running without data loss and supports overnight shift reporting.

How Offline Mode & Sync Work

1
Automatic Offline Queueing

If network connectivity drops, the Offline Sync Bar above the scanner switches to Offline Mode. Continue scanning badges normally—each scan is safely saved in the terminal's local queue with its exact local timestamp.

2
Automatic or Manual Upload on Reconnect

When internet returns, the terminal status flips back to Online. Pending scans sync automatically to the server, or you can click Sync Now to flush the queue immediately while preserving original scan times.

Important Offline Practice
Do not clear your browser site data or private-browsing session while scans are still marked as pending sync. Wait until the pending count reaches 0 and shows synced.

Generating Night-Shift & Multi-Day Attendance Reports

Above the User Activity Timeline on the Gate Dashboard, the Shift Report Toolbar lets supervisors filter and export attendance across day and overnight shifts:

Report Window When to Use & How to Export
Today (Live) Default real-time view of all check-ins, check-outs, and denials for the current operational day.
Night Shift (18:00–09:00) Captures overnight load-out, strike, and night-watch shifts spanning across midnight without splitting a shift into two separate days.
Last 7 Days Weekly punctuality review across multi-day conferences, concerts, or warehouse build weeks.
Custom Dates Pick exact From and To dates and click Apply for event-specific payroll or client audits.

Once filtered, click Export CSV to download a spreadsheet-ready file, or click Print to generate a clean printable shift attendance sheet.

6. Users, Badges, Gates & Shift Relief

Managing Staff, Roles & Clearance Matrix (Users & Roles)

  • Add a Team Member: Open Users & Roles and click + Add New User. Fill in full name, department, role, clearance tier, badge code (e.g., REW-BADGE-XXXX), emergency contact, and upload a clear headshot for gate visual verification.
  • Manage Roles, Tiers & Departments: Switch sub-tabs between Personnel Directory, Roles & Clearance Matrix CRUD, Clearance Matrix, and Departments to customize operational roles or export the full roster via Export Roster.

Managing Facility Gates & Event Venues

  • Facility Gates (Facilities & Gates): Administrators and Site Managers can click + New Facility Gate to register a warehouse or yard gate, set open/close hours, assign a controller contact, and set its dedicated Terminal PIN.
  • Venues & Checkpoints: View all active Venue and Facility gates, toggle Lock / Unlock Station, or jump directly to the live scanner via Launch Gate Terminal.

Shift Relief, Sick Leave & Night-Shift Handovers

Because Gate Controllers are strictly locked to a single assigned terminal, any handover or temporary coverage goes through a tracked approval workflow:

1
Open the Shift Relief Modal

Click Relief / Swap on the Gate Terminal header, or click Shift Relief & Swaps in the left sidebar.

2
Submit the Coverage Request

Select the Target Gate / Venue Checkpoint, choose the Relief Reason (Sick Leave Replacement, Shift Swap / Handover, High Workload Venue Backup, Night Shift Coverage, or New Terminal Assignment), select the Incoming Gate Controller, optionally set start/end times and notes, and click Submit Relief Request.

3
Administrator / Site Manager Approval

Approvers review the Pending & Recent Shift Relief Queue (highlighted by the amber badge count in the sidebar) and approve or decline. Once approved, the incoming controller is immediately bound to that checkpoint.

Common Gate Troubleshooting

Symptom Recommended Resolution
Terminal says "Screen Locked" Enter the checkpoint's dedicated Site PIN and click Unlock Terminal. If forgotten, ask an Administrator or Site Manager to check Site PINs.
Camera QR scanner won't start Ensure the terminal is unlocked first, allow camera permission in your browser prompt, and access the site over HTTPS or localhost. Use Flip Camera if the wrong lens opens.
Scan shows "Access Denied" Check the reason banner: the badge may belong to a clearance tier below the gate's requirement (e.g., L1 at a warehouse), be outside operating hours, or be inactive. Contact the Site Manager before permitting entry.
Internet dropped at the gate Keep scanning—the Offline Sync Bar automatically queues scans locally. Once signal returns, click Sync Now and confirm the pending counter drops to 0.
Controller needs to cover a different gate Gate Controllers cannot switch gates manually. Submit a request via Relief / Swap so an Administrator or Site Manager can approve the reassignment.
