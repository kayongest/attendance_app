2. The 4-Tier Security Clearance Matrix (L1 – L4)

Access rights in the aBility ecosystem are structured into four strictly governed clearance tiers. Each tier defines geographic perimeter allowances, warehouse custody rights, administrative capabilities, and badge color-coding.

Level 1 (L1) Event Venue Basic
Roles: Venue Crew, Hospitality, Drivers, Security Guard.
Venue Access: GRANTED (Assigned Venues)
Stock Warehouse Access: STRICTLY DENIED
  • Authorized for front-of-house, seating perimeters, and transit gates.
  • Turnstile scans at warehouse checkpoints immediately trigger "ACCESS RESTRICTED: L1 CLEARANCE".
  • Cannot hold equipment batch custody or sign for technical gear.
  • Read-only attendance records; zero system configuration rights.
Level 2 (L2) Technical & Stage Crew
Roles: Audio Techs, Sound Engineers, Lighting Techs, Video Directors, Rigging Leads.
Venue Access: FULL ACCESS (All Production Zones)
Stock Warehouse Access: AUTHORIZED (Gear Pickup & Return)
  • Unrestricted movement across Stage Left/Right, FOH, Dimmer Beach, and Truss perimeters.
  • Authorized to receive and return assigned equipment batches at warehouse kiosks.
  • Subject to strict call-time tracking: Auto-disabled if late without approval.
  • Profile links to active gear kits and tools currently in personal custody.
Level 3 (L3) Stock & Logistics Controller
Roles: Stock Controller, Warehouse Dispatcher, Tool-Crib Supervisor.
Venue Access: ASSIGNED LOGISTICS GATES
Stock Warehouse Access: FULL WAREHOUSE CUSTODY
  • Terminal Kiosk Operators: Authorized to run gate scans and badge validations.
  • Issues and receives equipment batches, performs serial verification and photo inspections.
  • Strict separation of duties: Cannot create or alter crew member accounts (prevents internal badge tampering).
  • Can flag broken/damaged gear and manage physical warehouse bin assignments.
Level 4 (L4) Executive & All-Access
Roles: Administrator, Technical Lead, Stock Logistics Manager, Project Director.
Venue Access: 24/7 ALL-ACCESS (All Venues)
Stock Warehouse Access: 24/7 ALL-ACCESS (All Warehouses)
  • Unrestricted security pass across every perimeter, vault, and control room.
  • Defines event Call-Times, shift cutoffs, and crew allocations.
  • Empowered with Emergency Override to reactivate late/blocked badges.
  • Full user provisioning, clearance tier adjustments, and payroll report certification.

Separation of Duties (Fraud & Tamper Protection)

Security Architecture: Why Stock Controllers Cannot Add Users
To prevent inside collusion where a warehouse staff member creates fake technician badges to check out expensive gear, Stock Controllers (L3) are hardcoded with zero user-creation privileges. User provisioning is strictly segregated to Technical Leads, Stock Managers, and Administrators (L4).

4. Dedicated Technician Punctuality Lifecycle

The foundation of a successful live production is punctuality. When an audio engineer or rigger is late, entire stage rehearsals stop. The aBility platform enforces a rigorous 4-State Punctuality State Machine designed to reward punctuality, enforce discipline on tardiness, support pre-approved exemptions, and mandate proof for absences.

State 1
ON TIME
Arrival within scheduled window (up to +15m grace). Full green badge admission.
State 2
LATE (DISABLE)
Arrival after cutoff (>15m late). Badge locked at gate. Supervisor override required.
State 3
WITH PERMISSION
Pre-approved late note or emergency exemption. Admitted with audit log flag.
State 4
MISSING / PROOF
No-show at shift cutoff. Hard suspension until verified documentary proof uploaded.
ON TIME Standard Punctual Admission & Immediate Operational Dispatch
GREEN LIGHT

Criteria: The technician scans at the gate kiosk between 60 minutes prior to Call-Time up to 15 minutes post Call-Time (Grace Window).

System Action:

  • Instant acoustic chime and green visual confirmation banner: "ACCESS GRANTED — ON TIME".
  • Attendance log record created with access_status = 'granted', punctuality_status = 'on_time'.
  • Technician status transitions from Ready to Checked In. Current location logged.
  • Equipment Unlocked: The warehouse tool-crib or stage runner is immediately authorized to release assigned microphone packs, beltpacks, or camera chains to this technician.
LATE (DISABLE) Automated Access Suspension & Supervisor Intervention Gate
SECURITY LOCKOUT

Criteria: The technician scans at the gate kiosk > 15 minutes after the scheduled Call-Time with no prior authorization logged.

System Action:

  • Automatic Badge Disable: Turnstile and kiosk scan instantly rejects entry with red visual alarm: "ACCESS RESTRICTED: LATE CALL-TIME — BADGE LOCKED".
  • Prevents unvetted technicians from sneaking onto live show stages unnoticed after safety briefings have concluded.
  • Equipment dispatch is blocked: Warehouse dispatchers cannot check out gear to locked badges.
  • Unlocking Protocol (Supervisor Override): To reactivate entry, the Lead Sound Engineer, Technical Lead, or Stage Manager (L4) must physically log into the supervisor terminal, enter authorization credentials, document the reason for late arrival, and execute an "Override & Admit" command. The override is permanently stamped in the audit trail.
WITH PERMISSION Pre-Authorized Arrival Adjustment & Shift Exemption
APPROVED OVERRIDE

Criteria: Technician notified production management in advance of unavoidable flight delay, gear transit delay, sound-check rescheduling, or doctor visit.

System Action:

  • Supervisor enters a "Permission Token" with approved expected arrival time into the management portal.
  • When the technician arrives and scans, the terminal reads the active permission token.
  • Kiosk projects: "ACCESS GRANTED — WITH PERMISSION (Authorized by Tech Lead)".
  • Badge is admitted cleanly; attendance record flags as with_permission so the technician is not penalized in disciplinary or punctuality scorecards.
MISSING — PROVIDE PROOF Unaccounted No-Show & Mandatory Documentary Evidence Workflow
MANDATORY PROOF AUDIT

Criteria: Scheduled technician fails to scan at any checkpoint before the call-time cutoff, without prior permission on record.

System Action:

  • Automated job tags technician profile as MISSING / NO-SHOW.
  • Supervisor dashboard immediately alerts the Technical Lead that a crew station (e.g., Camera 3 Operator) is unmanned, allowing immediate emergency stand-in reassignment.
  • Hard Suspension: The technician’s RFID badge is blacklisted from all venues until resolved.
  • Mandatory Proof Submission: To clear the flag and authorize timesheet hours, the technician must submit official documentary evidence (hospital medical certificate, police report, transport delay slip).
  • Management reviews proof -> If Approved: Record updated to "Excused Absence (Proof Verified)" and badge unblocked; If Denied: Confirmed as "Unexcused Absence" with formal disciplinary penalty.

5. Gate Terminal Kiosk (#pane-kiosk) & Equipment Sync

The interactive Gate Kiosk is the frontline defense of the client's venue operations. Designed for rapid, high-throughput scanning during morning call-in rushes (supporting up to 60 scans per minute), the terminal features a robust dual-checkpoint architecture and live equipment custody synchronization.

Interactive Gate Terminal Features

Terminal Feature Technical Functionality Client Operational Benefit
Dual Checkpoint Switcher Toggle seamlessly between Stock Warehouse (Ndera Warehouse, BK Arena Stock) and Event Venue (KCC Arena, Kigali Convention Centre). Same terminal hardware can be deployed at warehouse loading bays or stage security portals with location-specific clearance validation.
Instant Verification Pop-Up Modal triggers only on active badge scan: displays technician photo, badge code (REW-BADGE-XXXX), role, clearance color banner, and active call-time. Zero cognitive strain on security staff; instant visual match ensures no badge swapping or buddy punching.
Paired State Machine Enforces logical scan sequencing: Ready → Checked In → Checked Out. Disallows double check-in or phantom exits. Exact timesheet accuracy; prevents technicians from claiming hours while off-site.
Linked Equipment Batches Active equipment checked out to the technician (e.g., Fly Case #102, ATEM 1 M/E Switcher) displays directly inside the kiosk verification card. Security and warehouse staff verify all gear is returned before shift check-out is approved.

Equipment Photo Auto-Sync & Inventory Traceability

Equipment custody is directly integrated with the Stock Manager database. A major feature of the aBility inventory engine is Automatic Photo Propagation across Identical Assets:

  • When an inventory manager uploads a photo of an equipment model (e.g., Blackmagic UpDownCross HD or AJA UTAP SDI), the system automatically propagates the photo to all identical units in the warehouse sharing the same name and model.
  • Eliminates hours of manual photo uploads across dozens of identical fly cases and video converters.
  • When a technician checks out a batch, high-resolution equipment photos are displayed on the kiosk screen, ensuring security can verify the exact equipment package being removed from the warehouse.
Custody Chain Guarantee: Zero Unchecked Equipment Dispatches
An un-scanned or late technician whose badge is in the Disabled / Locked state is blocked from receiving equipment batches. The system guarantees that 100% of dispatched gear is legally bound to an active, verified, on-site technician.
